Features

Built around how
you actually live

Not a generic pill reminder. Axis is purpose-built for real medication routines — whether you manage two tablets or fourteen.

Smart Reminders

Intelligent alerts that adapt to your schedule. Critical medications get escalating notifications — with snooze support and missed-dose tracking.

Drug Interaction Checker

Scan your entire medication list for known interactions. Get clear severity ratings and plain-language explanations — no medical jargon.

Travel Packing Calculator

Planning a trip? Axis calculates exactly how many doses to pack, accounting for time zones, trip length, and carry-on requirements.

Adherence Calendar

A 30-day visual heatmap of your medication history. Spot patterns, track streaks, and share clean reports with your GP at your next appointment.

Refill Tracker

Never run out unexpectedly. Axis monitors your pill counts and sends refill alerts before you hit empty — with smart lead-time calculations.

Streaks & Achievements

Stay motivated with a streak system, milestone badges, and personalised weekly challenges — built to make consistency feel rewarding.
