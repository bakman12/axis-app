FAQ

Common questions

Because your PIN derives the encryption key, there's no way to reset it without losing encrypted data. Options:

  • If you set up an encrypted backup, restore from it via Settings → Backup & Restore
  • If biometric unlock is enabled, use Face ID or fingerprint to bypass the PIN
  • Otherwise, reset the app via Settings → Account → Reset App, then re-enter medications manually
  • Check notifications are enabled for Axis in your phone's Settings → Notifications
  • On Android, disable battery optimisation for Axis: Settings → Battery → Axis → Don't optimise
  • Open Axis and tap Settings → Notifications → Reschedule all
  • If you recently reinstalled, open the app once to re-register all reminders

Go to Settings → Backup & Export:

  • Encrypted backup — AES-encrypted file to iCloud or Google Drive
  • Export as PDF — printable adherence report for your doctor
  • Export as CSV — all dose logs as a spreadsheet

When you create a trip, Axis detects the destination time zone and offers:

  • Keep home times — reminders fire at the same clock time (good for fixed-interval meds)
  • Shift to local time — reminders shift to the equivalent local time (good for meal/sleep-based meds)

The packing calculator automatically accounts for travel days and any adjustment doses.
