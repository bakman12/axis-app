Security & Privacy

Your health data
stays yours. Always.

We built Axis with the assumption that health data is among the most sensitive data that exists. Here's how we protect it.

🔐

AES-256-GCM Encryption

Every sensitive field is encrypted on your device before it reaches our servers. The decryption key never leaves your phone.

👆

Biometric Unlock

Face ID, fingerprint, or PIN — every session requires authentication before any data is decrypted or displayed.

🔒

Auto-Lock on Background

Locks immediately when you switch apps and after 60 seconds of inactivity. No stale sessions left open.

🛡️

Screen Guard

Blocks screenshots and prevents your medication list from appearing in the app switcher or screen recordings.

📱

Hardware-Backed Keys

On iOS, keys are stored in the Secure Enclave. On Android, in the hardware Keystore. Root and jailbreak detected.

🚫

Zero Data Selling

We do not sell, rent, or share your health data with advertisers, insurers, or any third party. Ever. Full stop.
