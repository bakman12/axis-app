1 Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Medication data: names, dosages, schedules, and notes you enter
  • Dose logs: records of doses you mark as taken, missed, or skipped
  • Health profile: optional details such as health conditions, dietary restrictions, and goals
  • Mood and symptom logs: voluntary daily check-ins you choose to record
  • Travel information: trip destinations and dates used for packing calculations
  • Account information: email address used to create your account

Information collected automatically

  • Device identifiers: anonymous identifiers used to sync data across your devices
  • App usage data: anonymised crash reports and performance metrics
  • Notification interactions: whether reminders are acknowledged (not their content)

We do not collect precise location, microphone audio, camera images (unless you scan a barcode), or data from your contacts or other apps.

2 How we use your information

We use the information we collect solely to operate and improve Axis:

  • Scheduling and delivering medication reminders
  • Generating your adherence statistics and progress reports
  • Providing AI health coaching insights based on your own data
  • Calculating medication quantities for travel
  • Enabling data backup and cross-device sync
  • Diagnosing crashes and fixing bugs

We do not use your health data to train machine learning models, serve advertisements, or build profiles for third parties.

3 How we protect your data

On-device encryption

All sensitive fields — medication names, dosages, health conditions, notes, and trip details — are encrypted on your device using AES-256-GCM before being transmitted to our servers. The encryption key is derived from your PIN or biometric credential and never leaves your device.

Biometric authentication

Every session requires Face ID, fingerprint, or PIN verification before your data is decrypted. The app locks automatically after inactivity.

Transport security

All data in transit is protected by TLS 1.3. We enforce certificate pinning on mobile builds to prevent man-in-the-middle attacks.

Screen protection

The app prevents your medication list from appearing in the iOS/Android app switcher, system screenshots taken by other apps, and screen recordings.

Important: Because your encryption key is derived from your PIN/biometric, if you forget your PIN and lose your device it is not possible for us to recover your encrypted data. We strongly recommend enabling the encrypted backup feature.

4 Data sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: We use Base44 for backend infrastructure under strict data processing agreements.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or court order.
  • Safety: We may share information to prevent imminent physical harm.
  • Business transfer: In a merger or acquisition, your data would transfer under the same protections.

5 Data retention and deletion

We retain your data while your account is active. To delete your account and all data:

  • In the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account
  • By email: privacy@axishealth.app with subject "Account deletion request"

All data is permanently deleted within 30 days of a verified request.

6 Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your data. To exercise any right, contact privacy@axishealth.app. UK/EU residents may also contact the ICO at ico.org.uk.

  • Access — request a copy of your data
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data (most editable directly in-app)
  • Erasure — request deletion
  • Portability — export via Settings → Export
  • Restriction / Objection — limit or object to certain processing

7 Children's privacy

Axis is not directed at children under 13 (or 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us immediately and we will delete it.

8 Health data and NHS guidelines

Axis provides medication tracking and NHS-guideline information. This is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Always consult your doctor or pharmacist about your medications.

Health data is treated as special category data under UK GDPR and handled with the highest level of protection we apply.

9 Changes to this policy

When we make material changes we will update the date above, send an in-app notification, and for significant changes request re-acknowledgement. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
