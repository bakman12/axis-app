Security & Privacy

Your data.
Your device. Full stop.

Health data is the most sensitive data that exists. We built Axis on the assumption that only you should ever see it.

🔐

AES-256-GCM Encryption

Every field encrypted on-device before it touches our servers. The key never leaves your phone.

👆

Biometric Unlock

Face ID, fingerprint, or PIN required before any data is decrypted or displayed.

🔒

Auto-Lock

Locks instantly when you switch apps and after 60 seconds of inactivity.

🛡️

Screen Guard

Blocks screenshots and prevents your medication list from appearing in app switcher previews.

📱

Hardware-Backed Keys

iOS Secure Enclave and Android Keystore. Root and jailbreak detection active.

🚫

Zero Data Selling

We do not sell, rent, or share your health data with anyone. No exceptions.
